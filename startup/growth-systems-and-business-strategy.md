# Startup Growth Systems and Business Strategy Prompts

For founders building repeatable GTM and strategic decision systems.

## Prompt 1: Startup Growth Operating System

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a startup growth architect and GTM operator.

Task:
Design a 12-week growth operating system for an early-stage B2B SaaS startup targeting customer success teams.

Context:
- Stage: post-MVP, pre-PMF
- Team: 4 people
- Budget: $18,000 over 3 months
- Current traction: 22 trial users, 3 paid customers
- Goal: 25 paying customers by end of quarter

Style:
Execution-focused, metrics-driven, no fluff.

Constraints:
- Must include acquisition, activation, conversion, and retention loops
- Must include weekly experiment cadence
- Must define north-star metric and input metrics
- Must include founder time allocation model

Output Format:
1) Growth model overview
2) Weekly operating cadence
3) Experiment backlog template
4) Metrics scorecard
5) Team accountability framework
```

### Expected AI Output

- full-funnel growth structure with measurable actions
- practical experiment cycles with owner assignment
- metrics that map directly to PMF indicators

## Prompt 2: Business Strategy Decision Memo

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a strategy advisor for venture-backed SaaS founders.

Task:
Create a strategy memo that decides whether to expand upmarket or double down on SMB for the next 9 months.

Context:
- Current ARR: $240k
- Gross margin: 78%
- Churn: SMB 6.8% monthly, mid-market 2.1% monthly
- Sales motion: founder-led
- Product complexity: moderate, onboarding still manual

Style:
Board-level clarity with decision rigor.

Constraints:
- Include 3 strategic options with tradeoffs
- Include scenario analysis (base, optimistic, downside)
- Include resource implications per option
- Conclude with one recommendation and why

Output Format:
1) Strategic context
2) Option analysis table
3) Scenario modeling summary
4) Recommendation
5) First 60-day execution actions
```

### Expected AI Output

- option-level tradeoff clarity
- realistic resource and risk analysis
- decisive recommendation with implementation sequence
