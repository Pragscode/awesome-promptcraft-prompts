# Funnel and SEO Strategy Prompts

## Prompt 1: Full-Funnel Growth Strategy

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a growth marketing strategist specializing in B2B SaaS funnels.

Task:
Design an end-to-end marketing funnel strategy from awareness to paid conversion for an AI workflow SaaS product.

Context:
- Traffic sources: organic, LinkedIn, partner referrals
- Sales cycle: 21 to 45 days
- Goal: improve trial-to-paid conversion from 6% to 12%

Style:
Data-driven and implementation-focused.

Constraints:
- Include channel-specific messaging
- Include lead magnet strategy
- Include email lifecycle sequence
- Include attribution model recommendations

Output Format:
1) Funnel architecture
2) Messaging by stage
3) Campaign playbook
4) Attribution and analytics model
5) 8-week execution roadmap
```

### Expected AI Output

- full-funnel strategy with tactical detail
- practical lifecycle and conversion improvements
- measurable execution plan

## Prompt 2: SEO Content Cluster Planner

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are an SEO strategist focused on topical authority.

Task:
Create a 90-day SEO content cluster strategy for keywords around prompt engineering and AI productivity workflows.

Context:
- Domain authority: low to mid
- Team capacity: 4 articles/month
- Business goal: increase qualified organic traffic and newsletter signups

Style:
Strategic, keyword-intent aligned, and practical.

Constraints:
- Include pillar + cluster architecture
- Map search intent for each content asset
- Include internal linking strategy
- Include publication and refresh cadence

Output Format:
1) Topical map
2) Priority keyword clusters
3) Content calendar
4) Internal link framework
5) KPI and tracking plan
```

### Expected AI Output

- coherent topical authority strategy
- realistic output volume aligned to team capacity
- clear SEO execution sequence with metrics
