# LinkedIn Growth and Distribution Prompts

Use this pack to build an authority-driven LinkedIn growth system.

## Prompt 1: 30-Day LinkedIn Growth Engine

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a LinkedIn growth strategist for B2B experts.

Task:
Build a 30-day LinkedIn growth system for a founder sharing AI and SaaS execution insights.

Context:
- Current followers: 2,300
- Target audience: SaaS founders, operators, PMs
- Goal: +1,500 relevant followers and 3 inbound leads in 30 days
- Available time: 90 minutes/day

Style:
High-signal, anti-generic, authority-first.

Constraints:
- Include content pillars and post formats
- Include commenting and engagement workflow
- Include lead capture CTA strategy
- Include weekly analytics review process

Output Format:
1) Growth strategy overview
2) Weekly content calendar
3) Post framework templates
4) Engagement operating protocol
5) KPI dashboard and optimization loops
```

### Expected AI Output

- practical posting + engagement system
- specific frameworks for hooks, body, CTA
- optimization process based on real metrics

## Prompt 2: LinkedIn Post Quality Optimizer

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a social content editor specialized in B2B conversion writing.

Task:
Audit and improve my LinkedIn draft for clarity, authority, and engagement quality.

Context:
- Draft topic: why most AI automations fail in operations teams
- Audience: heads of ops, founders
- Goal: save/share quality over vanity likes

Style:
Direct and editor-level precise.

Constraints:
- Keep author voice intact
- Improve hook, structure, and CTA
- Remove fluff and weak claims
- Provide revised draft + rationale

Output Format:
1) Diagnostic feedback
2) Revised post
3) Change rationale
4) Optional A/B hook alternatives
```

### Expected AI Output

- meaningful structural improvements
- stronger argument flow and credibility
- concise, high-conviction revised post
