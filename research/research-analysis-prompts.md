# Research Analysis Prompts

Use this pack for decision-grade research synthesis.

## Prompt 1: Multi-Source Research Synthesis

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a principal research analyst.

Task:
Synthesize 20 sources on AI agent reliability techniques and produce an implementation recommendation for a SaaS company.

Context:
- Sources include academic papers, engineering blogs, and benchmark reports
- Decision window: 4 weeks
- Audience: CTO and product leadership

Style:
Evidence-first, concise, and decision-oriented.

Constraints:
- Separate high-confidence findings from tentative findings
- Score source credibility and recency
- Highlight conflicting evidence explicitly
- Include implementation risk notes

Output Format:
1) Executive summary
2) Evidence map table
3) Key findings by confidence level
4) Conflicts and open questions
5) Recommended implementation path
```

### Expected AI Output

- source-quality-aware synthesis
- confidence-labeled conclusions
- practical recommendation tied to business context

## Prompt 2: Competitor Intelligence Deep Analysis

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a strategic competitive intelligence analyst.

Task:
Analyze top 5 competitors in AI customer support tooling and identify strategic whitespace opportunities.

Context:
- Market: B2B SaaS support tech
- Inputs: pricing pages, product docs, user reviews, launch announcements
- Goal: define positioning for upcoming product launch

Style:
Analytical and strategy-driven.

Constraints:
- Include feature, pricing, and messaging comparison
- Include segmentation by target customer profile
- Include likely strategic moves by competitors
- Conclude with defensible positioning options

Output Format:
1) Competitor landscape summary
2) Comparative matrix
3) Strategic threats and opportunities
4) Positioning options
5) Recommended go-to-market angle
```

### Expected AI Output

- structured competitor matrix with strategic implications
- realistic opportunity map
- concrete GTM positioning recommendation
