# Prompt Engineering for Creators

Use these prompts to systematize content ideation and improve consistency.

## Prompt 1: Content Idea Quality Filter

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a content strategy analyst.

Task:
Evaluate and prioritize 20 content ideas for an AI productivity creator using impact, differentiation, and execution feasibility.

Context:
- Platform focus: LinkedIn + newsletter
- Audience: solo founders and knowledge workers
- Goal: maximize saves, shares, and email signups

Style:
Analytical and direct.

Constraints:
- Create a scoring model with weighted criteria
- Reject low-signal ideas explicitly
- Suggest improvements for weak ideas
- Recommend top 5 ideas to execute first

Output Format:
1) Scoring framework
2) Ranked ideas table
3) Improvement notes for weak ideas
4) 2-week execution shortlist
```

### Expected AI Output

- transparent scoring rationale
- strategic prioritization, not random idea selection
- execution-ready shortlist

## Prompt 2: Multi-Format Content Transformer

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are an AI content transformation specialist.

Task:
Transform one source article into a LinkedIn post, email newsletter intro, 5 tweet thread, and 60-second video script.

Context:
- Source topic: reducing hallucinations in AI support bots
- Target audience: startup operators and support leaders
- Tone: practical authority

Style:
Platform-native and concise.

Constraints:
- Preserve core insight across all formats
- Adapt hook and CTA per platform
- Avoid repetitive wording
- Keep factual claims intact

Output Format:
1) Core message summary
2) LinkedIn post draft
3) Newsletter intro draft
4) 5 tweet thread
5) 60-second script
```

### Expected AI Output

- consistent message with channel-specific adaptation
- strong hooks and clean calls-to-action
- coherent multi-channel narrative
