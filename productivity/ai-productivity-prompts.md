# AI Productivity Prompts

These prompts optimize how teams use AI for real work, not novelty tasks.

## Prompt 1: AI-Assisted Weekly Planning Copilot

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are an AI chief-of-staff assistant.

Task:
Convert my weekly goals, task backlog, and calendar constraints into an execution-ready plan.

Context:
- Goals: launch feature, publish one article, close 3 sales calls
- Team dependencies: design review on Tuesday, engineering deployment on Thursday
- Capacity: 42 focused work hours this week

Style:
Operationally precise and prioritization-heavy.

Constraints:
- Prioritize by leverage and deadline risk
- Include contingency buffers
- Include dependency-aware sequencing
- Include what not to do this week

Output Format:
1) Priority matrix
2) Weekly plan by day
3) Risk and dependency notes
4) Contingency plan
5) End-of-week review criteria
```

### Expected AI Output

- realistic plan with dependency logic
- explicit tradeoffs and scope control
- success criteria for weekly review

## Prompt 2: Team AI Workflow Optimizer

### Prompt

```text
Role:
You are an AI workflow consultant for small product teams.

Task:
Design a team-level AI productivity system across writing, coding support, customer analysis, and reporting.

Context:
- Team: 7 members across product, engineering, and marketing
- Current issue: ad hoc AI usage with inconsistent quality
- Goal: improve throughput while maintaining quality standards

Style:
Framework-driven and implementation-oriented.

Constraints:
- Define AI use cases by function
- Define quality checks before publishing outputs
- Include prompt library governance model
- Include enablement and adoption plan

Output Format:
1) Function-specific AI workflow map
2) Standard operating procedures
3) Quality and review checkpoints
4) Prompt library governance
5) 45-day rollout plan
```

### Expected AI Output

- cross-functional AI adoption framework
- guardrails to prevent low-quality AI outputs
- concrete rollout sequence and ownership
